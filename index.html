MORE THAN JUST A FEST

What is UTSAV '26?

UTSAV '26 is a college festival concept designed to bring together technology, Indian culture, artistic expression and student innovation.

The festival provides a platform for students to explore their interests, collaborate with others, share their talents and learn beyond the classroom.
